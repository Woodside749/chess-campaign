Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This page explains what Chess Campaign (chesscampaign.com) stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. In short: we only store what the game needs to work. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

Who is responsible

Chess Campaign is made and run by [FULL NAME], Denmark. Questions about your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Playing without an account

You can play without an account. Then nothing about you is sent to us: your progress and settings (like the volume) are saved only in your own browser, and you can remove them by clearing the website's data in your browser.

What we store when you create an account

  • Your email address and password, to log you in and to send account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password). Your password is stored scrambled (hashed); nobody can read it, not even us.
  • Your username, and the avatar colour and country you choose.
  • Your game progress: rounds won in the campaign, stars, titles and the bots you have beaten.
  • When your account was created, and that you confirmed you are at least 13.

Your username, avatar, country and title may be shown to other players (for example in the global chat or in online games, once those exist). Your email address is never shown to other players.

Why we store it

To give you the account you asked for: logging in, saving your progress across devices and sending account emails. (The legal basis is the agreement between you and us when you create an account, GDPR article 6(1)(b).)

Who helps us

We use a few services to run the game. They only handle your data to do this job for us:

  • Supabase stores the accounts and saved progress, on servers in [SUPABASE REGION].
  • Resend sends the account emails.
  • Netlify serves the website. Like most websites, its servers briefly record technical details such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the site safe.

These companies are based in the United States, so data may be handled outside the EU. They protect it with the EU's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Storage in your browser

The game saves a few things in your browser's storage: your progress, your settings and, when you are logged in, the login itself. This is only so the game works; there are no tracking or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time with the "Delete my account" button on your profile page. That deletes your account, username, profile and progress straight away.

Your rights

You may ask to see the data we have about you, to correct it, to get a copy of it, or to have it deleted, and you may object to how we use it. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Age

You need to be at least 13 years old to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
